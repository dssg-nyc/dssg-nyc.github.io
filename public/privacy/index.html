NYC x DSSG

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Data Diplomats for Nonprofits, Inc. ("we", "us") operates the NYC x DSSG website at dssg-nyc.github.io. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this site, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: the email address and message you submit. We use it to reply to you.
  • Newsletter: the email address and, if you provide it, your name, when you subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • Scheduling and events: if you book a meeting through our Google Calendar appointment page or register for an event listed on our Luma calendar, those services collect the details you enter.
  • Donations: if you donate through our Givebutter page, Givebutter processes your payment. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested.
  • Cookies and advertising identifiers: see the section below.

How we use information

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, send the newsletter and event information you asked for, manage bookings and volunteer matching, process donations, keep the site secure, and measure how the site is used. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

We rely on these providers, each of which handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree: contact form delivery
  • ConvertKit: newsletter subscriptions
  • Google: Google Calendar appointments and, once enabled, Google Ads and Google tag measurement
  • Luma: event calendar and registration
  • Givebutter: donations
  • GitHub: website hosting

Cookies, advertising and your choices

Where we use Google's advertising or measurement tags, they are loaded only after you choose "Accept" in our cookie banner. If you choose "Decline", we keep those tags in a restricted mode that does not set advertising cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's stored data in your browser, which will show the banner again.

You can also manage Google advertising personalization in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of many ad networks at aboutads.info.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict some processing. To make a request, email dssgnyc@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
